Common Challenges. Practical Solutions.

Every Business Faces Financial Challenges.

Here are some of the most common issues I help business owners address.

Cash Flow & Financial Control

Know where your cash stands and where it's going. I help you gain control through a 13-week cash flow forecast, accounts receivable analysis, accounts payable management, and practical plans to maintain financial stability.

A PRACTICAL FIRST STEP

A 13-Week Cash Flow Forecast

A rolling, weekly view of expected receipts, payments, and available cash to help you identify potential shortfalls early, prioritize payments, improve collections, and make informed decisions.

Financial Visibility & Reporting

Get reliable financial information and a clearer understanding of profitability, cash flow, and performance. I help implement meaningful reporting and key metrics so you know where the business stands and can make better decisions.

Profitability & Cost Management

Identify what's driving costs, evaluate pricing and margins, and improve operating performance. I help you find opportunities to increase profitability without compromising growth.

Tax & Financial Planning

Make better-informed decisions about tax obligations, owner compensation, investments, and other financial strategies before they become urgent problems, working alongside your existing tax advisors.

Growth & Important Decisions

Get experienced guidance on financing, expansion, acquisitions, ownership transitions, and other consequential decisions. I bring a practical, hands-on approach to help you move forward with confidence.

Systems & Operational Improvement

Improve processes, financial systems, controls, and reporting to reduce unnecessary work and help the business operate more effectively.
